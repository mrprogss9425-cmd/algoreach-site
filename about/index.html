About

Research with the receipts attached.

AlgoReach is an independent project led by Siddharth Singh. It began as a personal research engine for Indian equities and is being opened, by invitation, to the people it was built for.

What AlgoReach is

Research that shows its working.

AlgoReach reads exchange filings, news and market data for Indian and US listed equities, turns them into scored research signals, and writes every call to a hash-chained ledger that is scored against what the market did next. The record, including the misses, is part of the product.

What it does

01

Event intelligence

Material filings, results, deals and regulatory items are detected, classified and attributed to their source document, with the licence and fetch time on each record.

02

Crowd behaviour

Institutional and retail flow, block and bulk deals, derivatives positioning and narrative sentiment, measured over time rather than quoted as a mood.

03

Screening and research

Factor screens, sector heatmaps, company comparison, supply-chain propagation and a ticker workbench with financials, events and derivatives.

04

Portfolio and risk

Holdings exposure, concentration, event calendar for what you hold, stress scenarios and a frontier view, from a broker statement you import yourself.

05

Proof

An append-only ledger of every emitted call, scored at its horizon; hit rate and Brier score shown as measured, never as a promise.

06

Strategy lab

Rule-based strategies, model portfolios sized by a risk manager, paper trading. No live order routing.

How it is different

Most research products tell you what they think. AlgoReach also tells you how often it was right, on which event types, and how calibrated its probabilities were. When the evidence says there is no edge, the app says so.

  • Every figure opens a note: definition, source, owning page.
  • Every document carries its source URL, licence and hash.
  • Probabilities are calibrated against history and labelled uncalibrated when they are not.
  • New analytics ship switched off, run in shadow for at least five trading days, and are compared before they go live.

Who it is for

Retail investors who want to see the evidence, professional traders who need dense and fast screens, researchers who want a measured record, and fund teams who want a reproducible research process. AlgoReach is a research tool. It does not give investment advice and it does not place trades.

Access is by invitation during the beta. Requests are reviewed by hand.

What it reads

The data behind it

Example

A company posts a credit-rating upgrade on NSE at 11:05. The terminal shows it with the filing link and the time it was fetched; the price reaction is measured from that day's bars; a news story about it adds context but never replaces the filing.

Why AlgoReach does this

An event is only as good as its source. Reading the filing itself, and stamping when it was read, means every later step - the score, the reaction, the record - can be traced back to something you could open yourself.

Risk disclosure

AlgoReach provides research output: data, measurements, classifications and probabilities derived from public information. It is not investment advice, it is not tailored to your circumstances, and it does not place, route or recommend trades.

Investing in securities involves risk, including the loss of the capital invested. Probabilities on this site and in the terminal are estimates: a well-calibrated 70 percent call is still expected to be wrong about three times in ten. The record keeps every call, hits and misses alike, and the current record shows no proven edge.

Past measurements of the record do not indicate future results. Strategy and scenario figures are paper results under stated assumptions; they exclude slippage, market impact, fees and taxes unless a page says otherwise.
