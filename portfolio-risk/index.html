Portfolio & Risk

Know what you hold, and what can hurt it.

Import a broker statement and AlgoReach maps your book to sectors, factors and upcoming events, then shows how stated scenarios would move it. Nothing is executed; the book stays yours.

Exposure and diversification

  • Sector, factor and single-name concentration from your actual holdings.
  • Supply-chain propagation: which names in your book sit downstream of a shock.
  • The blindside board: events ahead on names you hold but are not watching.

Scenarios and drawdowns

Stress views show the book's move under named scenarios, in rupee crore and percent, with the sign convention stated on the panel. Paper-book equity and the deepest drawdowns are measured from the ledger.

Scenario results are model outputs under stated assumptions. They are not forecasts.

One name, in your book

What you see about a name you hold

Its events ahead, its exposure in your book, the assessment on it and the signals on the ledger, together on one page.

Hedging and sizing

Model portfolios are sized by a risk manager with a stated cap per name and a stated gross limit. Hedging views describe the exposure a hedge would offset; AlgoReach does not recommend or place a hedge.

Why the book is shown as is

Nothing is netted, smoothed or marked to a model without saying so, and no hedge is recommended. The purpose is to make what you hold legible, not to decide for you.

AlgoReach

Market intelligence for Indian equities, with the record kept in the open.

Invite-only beta

AlgoReach is a research engine for Indian and US listed equities, run by its founder from Mumbai. It reads public disclosures, news and market data, measures how the crowd behaves, and writes every conclusion to a ledger that is scored against what the market did next.

Questions, corrections and access requests go through the beta access form. There is no sales desk and no newsletter.

Risk disclosure

AlgoReach provides research output: data, measurements, classifications and probabilities derived from public information. It is not investment advice, it is not tailored to your circumstances, and it does not place, route or recommend trades.

Investing in securities involves risk, including the loss of the capital invested. Probabilities on this site and in the terminal are estimates: a well-calibrated 70 percent call is still expected to be wrong about three times in ten. The record keeps every call, hits and misses alike, and the current record shows no proven edge.

Past measurements of the record do not indicate future results. Strategy and scenario figures are paper results under stated assumptions; they exclude slippage, market impact, fees and taxes unless a page says otherwise.
