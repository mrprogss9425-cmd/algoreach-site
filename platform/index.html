Platform

Five workspaces, one engine.

The terminal is organised by the job you are doing, not by the data type. Each workspace has a one-screen overview and pages that go three or four clicks deep, on a phone, an iPad or a laptop.

Intelligence layers

  1. 01

    Sources

    Public exchange disclosures, regulators, news feeds, government statistics and market data, each with a health status and a licence tag.

  2. 02

    Documents

    De-duplicated, dated, entity-linked, with a provenance record per item.

  3. 03

    Events

    Material items classified by type, with the materiality bar stated in words on the page.

  4. 04

    Signals

    Research signals in a fixed schema with direction, horizon, probability and calibration bucket.

  5. 05

    Proof

    The ledger scores each signal at its horizon and publishes the running statistics.

Phone, tablet, desktop

On every device

The same engine, three shells: workspace homes with one-line briefs and live widgets on a phone, a rail and overview on an iPad, the full board on a laptop.

Workflows

Open the day with the brief, check what changed on names you hold, screen for the setups your rules describe, read the evidence behind an event, add names to a watchlist with alert conditions, and review your own record at the week's end.

AlgoReach

Market intelligence for Indian equities, with the record kept in the open.

Invite-only beta

AlgoReach is a research engine for Indian and US listed equities, run by its founder from Mumbai. It reads public disclosures, news and market data, measures how the crowd behaves, and writes every conclusion to a ledger that is scored against what the market did next.

Questions, corrections and access requests go through the beta access form. There is no sales desk and no newsletter.

Risk disclosure

AlgoReach provides research output: data, measurements, classifications and probabilities derived from public information. It is not investment advice, it is not tailored to your circumstances, and it does not place, route or recommend trades.

Investing in securities involves risk, including the loss of the capital invested. Probabilities on this site and in the terminal are estimates: a well-calibrated 70 percent call is still expected to be wrong about three times in ten. The record keeps every call, hits and misses alike, and the current record shows no proven edge.

Past measurements of the record do not indicate future results. Strategy and scenario figures are paper results under stated assumptions; they exclude slippage, market impact, fees and taxes unless a page says otherwise.
