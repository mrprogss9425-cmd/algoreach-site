Six layers

The method, layer by layer

Every layer is a place you can stop and check.

  1. 01

    Ingestion

    Public exchange disclosures, regulators, news feeds, government statistics and market data, each source with a health status and a licence tag.

    In practice

    A source that refuses automated access is not fetched. Each document carries its URL, fetch time, licence and content hash.

  2. 02

    Verification

    Documents are de-duplicated, dated and entity-linked; the data-QA pass flags gaps and incoherence before anything downstream reads them.

    In practice

    Sanity, reconciliation and coherence checks run in shadow and write their findings to the ledger.

  3. 03

    Quantitative analysis

    Event studies by type and horizon, factor screens on stored bars, crowd-behaviour series compared with what happened next.

    In practice

    Every study is pre-registered as a hypothesis and tested against a same-name placebo before it is kept.

  4. 04

    AI interpretation

    Where a language model drafts a rationale, the deterministic rules it summarises are shown beside it and the model is named.

    In practice

    The model never sets a probability; it explains one.

  5. 05

    Scoring

    Research signals in one fixed schema: direction, expected move, horizon, probability, calibration bucket, provenance.

    In practice

    A probability is labelled uncalibrated until enough history exists to calibrate it.

  6. 06

    Risk evaluation

    Model portfolios sized by a risk manager with stated caps; exposure, concentration and stress under named scenarios.

    In practice

    Nothing is executed. Paper trading only.

Explainable output

Every figure in the terminal opens a note with its definition, source and owning page. Scores carry their evidence, uncertainty and counter-case. Where a language model drafts a rationale, the deterministic rules it summarises are shown beside it, and the model is named.

Proprietary detectors and weights are not published. What is published is how they are tested and how they did.

Risk disclosure

AlgoReach provides research output: data, measurements, classifications and probabilities derived from public information. It is not investment advice, it is not tailored to your circumstances, and it does not place, route or recommend trades.

Investing in securities involves risk, including the loss of the capital invested. Probabilities on this site and in the terminal are estimates: a well-calibrated 70 percent call is still expected to be wrong about three times in ten. The record keeps every call, hits and misses alike, and the current record shows no proven edge.

Past measurements of the record do not indicate future results. Strategy and scenario figures are paper results under stated assumptions; they exclude slippage, market impact, fees and taxes unless a page says otherwise.
