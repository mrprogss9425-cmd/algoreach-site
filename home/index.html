What it does

01

Event intelligence

Material filings, results, deals and regulatory items are detected, classified and attributed to their source document, with the licence and fetch time on each record.

02

Crowd behaviour

Institutional and retail flow, block and bulk deals, derivatives positioning and narrative sentiment, measured over time rather than quoted as a mood.

03

Screening and research

Factor screens, sector heatmaps, company comparison, supply-chain propagation and a ticker workbench with financials, events and derivatives.

04

Portfolio and risk

Holdings exposure, concentration, event calendar for what you hold, stress scenarios and a frontier view, from a broker statement you import yourself.

05

Proof

An append-only ledger of every emitted call, scored at its horizon; hit rate and Brier score shown as measured, never as a promise.

06

Strategy lab

Rule-based strategies, model portfolios sized by a risk manager, paper trading. No live order routing.

The Intelligence Observatory

How it reads one name

Open the Observatory on any name and the engine shows what it read: the event, how the crowd reacted, who the company is, where institutions moved, the assessment it produced, the signals on the ledger and what that means for your book.

How it is different

Most research products tell you what they think. AlgoReach also tells you how often it was right, on which event types, and how calibrated its probabilities were. When the evidence says there is no edge, the app says so.

  • Every figure opens a note: definition, source, owning page.
  • Every document carries its source URL, licence and hash.
  • Probabilities are calibrated against history and labelled uncalibrated when they are not.
  • New analytics ship switched off, run in shadow for at least five trading days, and are compared before they go live.

Who it is for

Retail investors who want to see the evidence, professional traders who need dense and fast screens, researchers who want a measured record, and fund teams who want a reproducible research process. AlgoReach is a research tool. It does not give investment advice and it does not place trades.

How a call is scored

A call names its direction, horizon and stated probability when it is written. When the horizon arrives it is scored against the market, hit or miss, and the result is appended. Calibration then compares what was stated with what was measured.

  1. The track record lists every emitted call with its horizon and its score; misses stay.
  2. Calibration: stated probability on one axis, measured frequency on the other, bucket by bucket.
  3. A bucket far from the diagonal is a bucket the engine has to earn; the page says so.

The method

Six layers, each one checkable

From raw disclosure to a scored, risk-aware research signal. Open a layer to see what it means in practice.

  1. 01

    Ingestion

    Public exchange disclosures, regulators, news feeds, government statistics and market data, each source with a health status and a licence tag.

    In practice

    A source that refuses automated access is not fetched. Each document carries its URL, fetch time, licence and content hash.

  2. 02

    Verification

    Documents are de-duplicated, dated and entity-linked; the data-QA pass flags gaps and incoherence before anything downstream reads them.

    In practice

    Sanity, reconciliation and coherence checks run in shadow and write their findings to the ledger.

  3. 03

    Quantitative analysis

    Event studies by type and horizon, factor screens on stored bars, crowd-behaviour series compared with what happened next.

    In practice

    Every study is pre-registered as a hypothesis and tested against a same-name placebo before it is kept.

  4. 04

    AI interpretation

    Where a language model drafts a rationale, the deterministic rules it summarises are shown beside it and the model is named.

    In practice

    The model never sets a probability; it explains one.

  5. 05

    Scoring

    Research signals in one fixed schema: direction, expected move, horizon, probability, calibration bucket, provenance.

    In practice

    A probability is labelled uncalibrated until enough history exists to calibrate it.

  6. 06

    Risk evaluation

    Model portfolios sized by a risk manager with stated caps; exposure, concentration and stress under named scenarios.

    In practice

    Nothing is executed. Paper trading only.

Risk disclosure

AlgoReach provides research output: data, measurements, classifications and probabilities derived from public information. It is not investment advice, it is not tailored to your circumstances, and it does not place, route or recommend trades.

Investing in securities involves risk, including the loss of the capital invested. Probabilities on this site and in the terminal are estimates: a well-calibrated 70 percent call is still expected to be wrong about three times in ten. The record keeps every call, hits and misses alike, and the current record shows no proven edge.

Past measurements of the record do not indicate future results. Strategy and scenario figures are paper results under stated assumptions; they exclude slippage, market impact, fees and taxes unless a page says otherwise.

  • Data. shown under the terms of its owners, with source, time and licence on each item; not for redistribution
