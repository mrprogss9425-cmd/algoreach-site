Event intelligence

Filings and announcements are classified by event type and scheduled-versus-surprise. Each material event is studied against the price path that followed, by event type and horizon, and the study is the prior behind the probability on a new signal of that type.

From information to a scored signal

What a signal is made of

An event lands, the crowd reacts and is measured, fundamentals and the macro backdrop frame it, and a scored signal comes out with its reasons, its uncertainty and its counter-case.

Crowd behaviour

Institutional and retail flow, block and bulk deals, derivatives build-up and narrative sentiment are stored as time series and compared with what happened next. The divergence and saturation measures are shown with their coverage, so a thin series is visibly thin.

Screening and scoring

Factor screens run on stored bars with their data age shown. Scores carry the evidence that produced them, an uncertainty statement and the counter-case, because a score without its reasons is just a number.

The record

Each emitted signal is appended to a hash-chained ledger with the probability stated at the time. At the horizon it is scored against the market. The app shows hit rate, Brier score against the stated probability, and the comparison with a same-name placebo. The current record shows no proven edge, and the app says exactly that.

  • Pre-registered hypotheses, with the registration date on the result.
  • Walk-forward tests per signal family against a placebo.
  • Failed ideas are kept on a list and are not re-tuned until they pass.

Risk disclosure

AlgoReach provides research output: data, measurements, classifications and probabilities derived from public information. It is not investment advice, it is not tailored to your circumstances, and it does not place, route or recommend trades.

Investing in securities involves risk, including the loss of the capital invested. Probabilities on this site and in the terminal are estimates: a well-calibrated 70 percent call is still expected to be wrong about three times in ten. The record keeps every call, hits and misses alike, and the current record shows no proven edge.

Past measurements of the record do not indicate future results. Strategy and scenario figures are paper results under stated assumptions; they exclude slippage, market impact, fees and taxes unless a page says otherwise.
