What is being trained

01

Calibration

Calibration: every call reaching its horizon is scored and feeds the calibration buckets.

02

Coverage

Coverage: source health is checked daily and thin series are flagged until they lengthen.

03

Fits

Fits: crowd-behaviour hypotheses run in shadow and are kept only after an out-of-sample check.

04

Resilience

Resilience: a stopped session names its cause and resumes without losing its record.

AlgoReach

Invite-only beta

AlgoReach is a research engine for Indian and US listed equities, run by its founder from Mumbai. It reads public disclosures, news and market data, measures how the crowd behaves, and writes every conclusion to a ledger that is scored against what the market did next.

Questions, corrections and access requests go through the beta access form. There is no sales desk and no newsletter.

Risk disclosure

AlgoReach provides research output: data, measurements, classifications and probabilities derived from public information. It is not investment advice, it is not tailored to your circumstances, and it does not place, route or recommend trades.

Investing in securities involves risk, including the loss of the capital invested. Probabilities on this site and in the terminal are estimates: a well-calibrated 70 percent call is still expected to be wrong about three times in ten. The record keeps every call, hits and misses alike, and the current record shows no proven edge.

Past measurements of the record do not indicate future results. Strategy and scenario figures are paper results under stated assumptions; they exclude slippage, market impact, fees and taxes unless a page says otherwise.
